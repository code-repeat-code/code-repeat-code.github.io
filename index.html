About

Most of my work sits between a message arriving and a correct record landing somewhere a person can trust it.

I joined Advisor360 in 2022 as a Software Engineer 1, fixing production issues for an application with more than 20,000 users and redesigning the stored procedures behind financial record matching. Over the next three years the problems grew: Kafka microservices sustaining over a million messages a second, a reporting job that took nineteen minutes and needed to take seconds, and a codebase that had to become easier to extend without breaking.

Since December 2025 I've been a Software Engineer 3, owning the design of the orchestration platform above and setting technical direction through design reviews and mentoring — service boundaries, error handling, and testing standards. I care about the unglamorous parts: idempotency, test coverage in the pipeline, and the query plan nobody looked at.
